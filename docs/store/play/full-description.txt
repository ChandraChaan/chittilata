Chittilata (చిట్టిలాట) brings two much-loved Telugu paper-chit games to your phone: Movie Chits (Chittilu Aata) and King & Queen. No paper, no pen, no scissors. Everyone plays on their own phone, and every chit stays secret.

🎬 MOVIE CHITS (3–10 players)
• One person creates a room and shares the 6-letter code.
• Everyone picks four folded movie chits.
• Pass one slip at a time around a random circle. Keep what you need, pass on what you don't.
• The first to collect four of the same name wins the round!
• Everyone else picks a secret character chit: Queen, Police, Thief and more, each worth different points.
• Play 7, 10, 15 or 20 rounds. The top scorer is crowned King and the runner-up is Queen.

👑 KING & QUEEN (4–10 players)
• Pick one folded chit to get your secret role: King, Queen, Police, Thief, plus Minister, Commander and more for bigger groups.
• The King must find the Queen, the Queen finds the Police, and so on down the chain.
• Guess wrong and you swap roles!
• Everyone scores the role they end with. Most points after all rounds wins.

🎉 MADE FOR FAMILY GAME NIGHTS
• Telugu movies by default, plus All-Indian, Hollywood, Mixed, or your own custom names: cities, cricketers, anything!
• Easy, Normal and Challenging difficulty.
• A turn timer with a live countdown everyone sees. No more waiting on that one cousin!
• Folded-paper design, celebrations, paper sounds and gentle vibration, each with its own on/off switch.
• Light and dark themes, and a history of your past games.
• Quick chat: tap an emoji or a fun phrase (త్వరగా! ⏰ అయ్యో! 😩 Good game! 🤝) and it floats up on everyone's screen.
• Someone taking forever? Nudge them 👋 and their phone buzzes. Nothing is ever played for you automatically.

🔒 FAIR AND PRIVATE
• Every deal is shuffled fairly on our server, and nobody starts close to winning.
• Only you can see your slips and your role. Not even the host can peek.
• Every pick, pass and guess is checked by the server, so nobody can cheat.
• Lost connection? Reopen the app and you're right back in your seat.
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No sign-up: just pick a name and play.

Gather the family. Pick the chits. Wear the crown. 👑
