ChitPlay is the paper-chit party game for friends and family: Movie Chits, King & Queen and Movie Dumb Charades. No paper, no pen, no scissors. Everyone plays on their own phone, and every chit stays secret.

Play with English movies, or switch to Telugu, all-Indian or mixed movies in one tap. The classic Indian chit games (Chittilu Aata, Raja Rani) your family grew up with, now for everyone at the table.

🎬 MOVIE CHITS (3–10 players)
• One person creates a room and shares the 6-letter code or an invite link.
• Everyone picks four folded movie chits.
• Pass one slip at a time around a random circle: tap it, or drag it onto the circle and watch it fly to the next player. Keep what you need, pass on what you don't.
• The first to collect four of the same name wins the round!
• Everyone else picks a secret character chit: Queen, Police, Thief and more, each worth different points.
• Play 7, 10, 15 or 20 rounds. The top scorer is crowned King and the runner-up is Queen.

👑 KING & QUEEN (4–10 players)
• Pick one folded chit to get your secret role: King, Queen, Police, Thief, plus Minister, Commander and more for bigger groups.
• The King must find the Queen, the Queen finds the Police, and so on down the chain.
• Guess wrong and you swap roles!
• Everyone scores the role they end with. Most points after all rounds wins.

🎭 MOVIE DUMB CHARADES (3–10 people together)
• One player secretly gets a movie and acts it out without a word.
• Everyone else picks the title from six on their phone. One guess each!
• The fastest right guess scores most, and the actor scores too. Everyone takes turns acting.
