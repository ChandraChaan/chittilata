# Chittilata – ChitPlay 2.4.0 (build 9)

Chittilata now carries **ChitPlay** as its global name and is English-first everywhere, so the whole table can play. The package name, app id and Play listing are unchanged.

## New

- **Room chat.** A chat panel with the room's history, 16 emoji reactions, 20 fun preset lines (English first, with the Telugu classics kept) and, in rooms of friends, short typed messages. Quick-match tables with strangers stay preset-only. Unread messages show on the chat button.
- **Drag and drop.** Drag a folded chit down into your tray to pick it. Drag a slip onto the circle to pass it, or drop it on another slip to rearrange your hand. Tapping still works.
- **A passing screen built to be stared at.** No scrolling. The countdown and lap count sit in the middle of the circle, every pass sends a slip visibly flying to the next seat, the current player's seat glows, every seat shows its score, and the movie chips show how many of each you hold.
- **Join a running game.** Arrive late? Take a free seat and play from the next round.
- **Host controls, at any time.** End the game for everyone (the current round is cancelled and the points so far decide the King and Queen), warn a quiet player, remove players during a game, or close a lobby. Anyone can leave from any screen.
- **"Are you still there?" warnings.** A warned player gets 60 seconds to tap *I'm here!* (any move counts). If they don't, they're removed automatically. After two warnings the host can remove them at once. The warning is also pushed to a closed app.
- **No more stalemates.** Four laps of passing with no winner? The host can end the round: everyone draws a character chit, and one of them is a King worth the win points.

## Fixed

- The last character chits no longer ignore taps after a quick fourth movie pick.
- No more black screen after *Return to home*, even with a slow connection or a double tap.
- The last round's results are shown before the King and Queen are announced, and in King & Queen the final sure guess is made by the player, not the server, so no round ends before everyone has played.

## Changed

- Removed players are told why, and players removed for not responding may rejoin.
- Scores count up instead of jumping, "your turn" glows, and the final screen explains an early end.
