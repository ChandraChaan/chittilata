# How to play Chittilata — quick guide

Chittilata has two games. Pick one on the home screen: **🎬 Movie Chits** or **👑 King & Queen**. Everyone else taps **Join with a code**.

---

## 🎬 Movie Chits

A two-minute guide for friends and family. The complete rules are in [GAME_RULES.md](GAME_RULES.md). A video walkthrough is in [`media/chittilata-how-to-play.mp4`](media/chittilata-how-to-play.mp4).

## 1. Get everyone in one room

1. Everyone installs Chittilata and types their name.
2. **One person** taps **Create a room**. A code like `MQPYTM` appears. Tap **Copy invite** and send it to your group chat.
3. Everyone else taps **Join with a code**, enters the code, then taps **I'm ready**.
4. The host can tap **Edit** to choose the category, difficulty, timer and number of rounds, then taps **Start game**.

## 2. Pick your chits

Watch the names get written, folded and shuffled onto the mat. When the timer appears, **tap any 4 folded chits**. Each one unfolds just for you and drops into your tray.

## 3. Watch the circle

Each round opens with the **passing circle**: who goes first and who passes to whom. It changes every round.

## 4. Pass your slips

- Your 4 slips are at the bottom of the screen. Only you can see them.
- When the banner says **Your turn**, tap the slip you want to give away, then tap **Pass to …**.
- When a slip comes to you it drops in with a **NEW** tag, and you hold 5 slips. Keep the ones you want and pass one on.
- Slips you hold three of get a **★**. Aim for four of the same name.
- Keep an eye on the countdown ring. If it runs out, a slip is passed for you.

## 5. Four of a kind wins

Hold four matching slips and you win the round: **+1,000 points** and confetti. Everyone else **picks one folded character chit** and unfolds a secret character such as Queen, Police or Thief, worth different points.

## 6. Reveal, score, repeat

All hands and characters are revealed, the leaderboard updates, and the host starts the next round.

## 7. Crown the King and Queen

After the last round, the top score is **King** and the second is **Queen**. Tap **Play again** (host) for another game with the same group.

### Tips

- Keep the screen on during a game. Chittilata keeps it awake for you.
- Turn sound, music and vibration on or off in **Settings**.
- If your connection drops, just reopen the app. Your seat is saved.

## 👑 King & Queen

Full rules and the state model: [KING_QUEEN_RULES.md](KING_QUEEN_RULES.md).

1. **Create a King & Queen room** and share the code. You need 4–10 players. The host can set the rounds, the time per guess and every role's points.
2. **Pick one folded chit.** It unfolds privately to show your role: King, Queen, Police or Thief, plus Minister, Commander and more in bigger games. Keep a straight face!
3. **The King is announced** and must find the **Queen**. On your turn, tap the player you suspect and confirm **"Is … the Queen?"**
4. **Right guess:** you keep your role for good, and the player you found must now find the next role in the chain.
5. **Wrong guess:** you two **swap roles** and you lose your turn. They now hold your role and keep hunting. Players already ruled out are marked "not the …".
6. When everyone is found, all roles are revealed and **everyone scores their final role**. After the last round, the top total is King and the next is Queen. Ties go to whoever finished the most rounds as King.
