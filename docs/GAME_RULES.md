# Chittilata (చిట్టిలాట) — Movie Chits rules

> These are the rules for **🎬 Movie Chits**. For **👑 King & Queen** see [KING_QUEEN_RULES.md](KING_QUEEN_RULES.md).

Chittilata is the traditional Telugu paper-slip game **Chittilu Aata** for phones. Every player uses their own Android phone, and the slips stay private.

## Players

- 3 to 10 players. Four players is the classic game.
- One player **creates a room** and shares the 6-character **room code**. The others **join with the code** and tap **I'm ready**.
- The host chooses the settings and starts the game once everyone is ready.

## The slips — picking folded chits

Each round uses **one name per player**, with **four slips of each name**. A four-player game has 4 names × 4 slips = 16 slips.

**Making the chits.** At the start of the round every phone shows the same thing: the names are written on paper slips, folded, gathered into one pile and shuffled, then scattered across a woven mat.

**Picking.** Every player taps **4 folded chits**, in any order, at the same time as everyone else. Each chit you pick unfolds privately on your phone. A chit can only be picked once; if two players reach for the same chit, the first one wins and the other picks again. You have 25 seconds. When time is up, any chits you still need are picked for you at random.

**Fair by design.** Before anyone picks, the server deals every player a fair, random hand:

- With **4 or more players**, every player starts with **4 different names**.
- With **3 players** there are only 3 names, so each hand is the most varied possible: one name twice and the other two once each.
- Nobody ever starts with three or four of the same name, so nobody can win before passing begins.

Each chit you pick is bound by the server to the next slip of your own fair hand. So **which chit you pick doesn't change your chances** (nobody can gain an edge by picking) while you still get to choose and unfold your own chits. Only you can see your slips. The game enforces this on the server, not just by hiding them on screen, and the host has no extra access.

## The passing circle

Before each round, a **new random circle** is shown. It tells you who passes first, the order, and the direction. The circle stays fixed for the whole round.

1. The first player passes **one** slip to the next player in the circle.
2. That player now holds **5 slips**. They look at the new slip (marked **NEW**) and pass **any one** of their five to the next player, going back to four.
3. The next player does the same, and so on around the circle, again and again.

You can only pass on your turn, only one slip per turn, and only a slip you actually hold. The first player briefly holds 3 slips until the slip travelling around the circle comes back to them.

## The turn timer

The host picks a timer: 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds, or **No timer**. The countdown comes from the server, so every phone shows the same time, even after reconnecting.

If time runs out, the game passes a slip **for** you and tells everyone:

- **Pass the slip I just received** (default). If you had not received one yet, a random slip is passed.
- **Pass a random slip** (the host can choose this instead).

Neither rule ever looks at which slip would help you, so a timeout never helps you. With **No timer**, the game waits 5 minutes before passing for you, so an idle phone can't freeze the game.

## Winning a round

The first player to hold **4 slips with the same name** wins the round and earns the **movie-win points** (1,000 by default).

- If you hold 5 slips and four of them match, pass the odd one out. You win the moment you are back to four matching.
- Passing stops immediately and the server records exactly **one** winner. If one pass completes two hands at once, the player who passed wins.

## Secret characters

The other players then pick their **character chits**. One character is written on each folded chit (for example Queen 800, Police 500, Thief 100), sealed and shuffled by the server. Each non-winner taps **one** chit and it unfolds privately. **The character inside is exactly what you get.** Nobody sees anyone else's character until the reveal. You have 20 seconds, then any remaining chits are handed out at random. The winner just watches.

| Players in game | Characters used (default points) |
|---|---|
| 3 | Queen 800, Thief 100 |
| 4 | Queen 800, Thief 100, Police 500 |
| 5 | + Minister 700 |
| 6 | + Commander 600 |
| 7 | + Merchant 400 |
| 8 | + Poet 300 |
| 9 | + Farmer 200 |
| 10 | + Jester 150 |

The host can rename characters and change their points. Characters are used from the top of the list. The host must keep at least one character per non-winning player, or the game won't start.

## Reveal and scoring

A few seconds later, everyone's slips and characters are revealed. Each player's round points are added to their total, and the leaderboard updates. The host then starts the next round, which gets new names, a new deal and a new circle.

## Rounds

The host picks 7, 10, 15 or 20 rounds, or any number from 1 to 30.

## King and Queen

After the last round:

- The highest total is **King** and the second-highest is **Queen**.
- **Ties** are settled by most movie wins.
- **Still tied for a title?** The tied players are asked whether to play a **tie-breaker**:
  - **Everyone tied accepts:** one extra round is played between **only the tied players**, with new names, a new deal and a new circle (it works with just two players). Everyone else watches. The tie-breaker winner ranks first among them, and the others rank by their tie-breaker character points. These points **only break the tie**; they are never added to anyone's total.
  - **Anyone chooses "Share the title"**, or nobody answers within 30 seconds: the scores stay as they are and the tied players **share** the title and rank, for example "Joint Kings".
  - If a tied player leaves, the title is shared.
- The King tie is resolved first. If the King is clear but the Queen is tied, the vote is about Queen. Only one tie-breaker is offered per game, and if the tie-breaker itself ends level, the title is shared.

## Categories and difficulty

**Categories:** Telugu movies (default), All Indian movies, Hollywood movies, Mixed, or **Custom names**. Custom names can be anything, such as countries, cities, fruits or celebrities, and you need at least one name per player.

| Difficulty | Names used | Default timer |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | Well-known titles | 30 s |
| Normal | Well-known plus recent (2015+) titles | 20 s |
| Challenging | The whole catalogue | 10 s |

Difficulty never changes the rules or the fairness of the deal. The game avoids repeating names within one game until it runs out of fresh ones.

## Leaving and reconnecting

- **Losing connection or closing the app does not remove you.** Reopen Chittilata and you return to your seat with the right slips and timer. While you're away, your turns time out.
- **Leaving** during passing **cancels the round** for everyone (no points), and it still counts as one of the rounds. If fewer than 3 players remain, the game ends with the scores so far.
- In the lobby, the host can remove a player, who then can't rejoin that room.
- Rooms close after 24 hours without activity.
