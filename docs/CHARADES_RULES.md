# Movie Dumb Charades

A party game for 3–10 people **in the same room** (or on a video call). Everyone uses their own phone.

## How a round works

1. One player is the **actor**. Players take turns, in the order they joined the room.
2. The actor's phone shows a **secret movie title**. Nobody else can see it, not even the host: the server keeps the answer until the round ends.
3. After 5 seconds to get ready, the actor has **75 seconds** to act it out:
   - no talking, no lip-syncing, no humming the song;
   - no pointing at things with letters or spelling in the air;
   - fingers for the number of words and syllables, then act each word or the whole story.
4. Everyone else sees **six titles** on their phone and taps the one they think it is. **One guess each**, so watch first.
5. The round ends at the **first right guess**, when everyone has guessed wrong, or when time runs out.

## Scoring

| Who | Points |
|---|---|
| First right guess | **100 + 2 per second left** (up to 250) |
| The actor, if someone got it | **75** |
| Wrong guess, or nobody got it | 0 |

After the last round (the host picks 3–15) the top two are crowned **King** and **Queen**. Ties go to whoever guessed right most often; players still tied share the title.

## Good to know

- Bots can't join Charades: they can't act.
- If the actor leaves mid-round, that round ends without points.
- People who join after the start watch, and get a turn when the game is played again.
